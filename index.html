About

Data-driven operations for consequential settings

I am an Assistant Professor of Operations Management at the University of Colorado Boulder. My research focuses on data-driven problems in platform operations, environmental sustainability, and healthcare operations.

I received my PhD in Supply Chain, Operations, and Technology from Washington University in St. Louis, where I was advised by Professor Dennis Zhang. I also hold an A.M. in Statistics from Washington University in St. Louis and a B.A. in Mathematics from the University of Cambridge.
